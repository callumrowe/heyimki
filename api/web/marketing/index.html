Why Ki is different

Built for one. Not a million.

Every AI product on the market is optimised for the average user. Ki is optimised for you.

  • Yours, not everyone's
    No product roadmap. No feature requests. No waiting for the next update. Ki is shaped around one person — and that person is you.
  • Actually has a personality
    Warm, direct, occasionally sweary. Ki has opinions, pushes back when it's worth it, and notices things without being asked. Not a yes machine.
  • Shows up first
    A heartbeat runs around the clock. Ki checks your open loops, upcoming commitments, and anything worth flagging — and messages you before you have to think about it.
  • Your data never leaves your server
    Self-hosted, private, yours. No third party logging your conversations, mining your context, or reading your notes.
  • Builds its own tools
    When Ki needs a new capability, it writes it and ships it. You describe what you want. Done.
Under the hood

Your server. Your data. Full stop.
